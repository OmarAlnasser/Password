What's new in 0.1.2

- Browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave (Windows): fill your logins on websites, and save new logins or changed passwords to Khazna after you sign in. Turn it on in Settings > Browser extension, which also shows how to add it to your browser.
- The browser has to be paired first: Khazna shows a code and asks you to allow it. Nothing is filled or saved without your click.
- Browser activity: every fill, save and password change made from the browser is listed, with a badge for new ones.
