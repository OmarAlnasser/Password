What's new in 0.1.1

- New name: the app is now called Khazna (خزنة). Your vault and settings stay as they are.
- Select several items at once: long-press an item (or use Select in the menu), tick the ones you want, then delete them together.
- Sort by "Recently used": the passwords you copy or fill most recently come first. "Name (A–Z)" and "Recently added" are still there.
- Passwords stay hidden: every password is shown as dots until you press the eye, and it hides again by itself after 15 seconds.
- Switching between English and Arabic now takes effect immediately.
